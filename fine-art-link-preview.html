About

Building at the intersection of culture, design, and technology.

From concept to culture, I've spent over 20 years building creative systems, designing product, leading high-output teams, and shaping experiences that last. My foundation is fine art, which means I think in terms of form, weight, and tension before I think in terms of features. That shapes everything I make, and it's the reason the work tends to feel considered rather than assembled. Rooted in a deep understanding of the human experience, I identify cultural signals early and use emerging technologies to create work that people return to, reference, and remember.
